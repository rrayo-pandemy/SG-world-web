# Frontend home surface

## Scope

Homepage redesign for the existing web storefront, in Persuade mode. Visitors are homeowners and people responsible for business spaces. They should understand the range of general services and remember the company. The primary action is to explore the work; existing phone and WhatsApp links remain secondary contact paths.

The user explicitly pinned a vertical "scroll-world" experience, the general company name, Spanish copy, image motion, and preservation of all existing system behavior. Keep authentication, roles, account flows, cart, product rendering, API hooks, theme control and existing contact destinations intact. Do not introduce unsupported claims, certifications, portfolio work, prices or guarantees. The user asked for plumbing, water/drain pipes and electrical installations; additional service lines remain proposals until company scope is confirmed.

## Direction contract

**THESIS:** Make the visit feel like moving through a working building: each scroll reveals a different trade and its effect on the space. Refuse the generic service-card catalogue.

**OWN-WORLD:** Use a mineral white ground, deep evergreen/graphite work surfaces and a single high-visibility citron accent. Pair existing display typography with technical labels, measured linework and actual sourced trade photography. Keep the visual language coherent across navigation, service chapters and footer.

**STORY:** First show the company and its scope; then move through water, electricity, construction/finishes and ongoing maintenance; close with ways to reach the business. Photos are illustrative stock, never claimed as company projects.

**FIRST VIEWPORT:** Full-height image-led arrival with visible "SERVICIOS GENERALES", a short promise about installation and maintenance, and a direct "Explorar servicios" action. Navigation and account/theme controls remain legible above it.

**FORM:** User-pinned scroll-world: one continuous vertical sequence with restrained image drift, an editorial service index, alternating full-width scenes and a clear final contact close. The user specified the form; no concept-seed key was assigned.

**FINISH:** unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
